01 // ABOUT THE BUILDER

Not a polished origin story. More like an active build log.

WHY I BUILD

I like the point where a weird idea has to become understandable.

That usually means taking an unreliable input, deciding what matters, building a clear workflow, and being honest about what still breaks. Studying Computer Science and Business Administration keeps me interested in both sides: how the system works and why anyone would want it.

My projects are prototypes, experiments, and working tools—not imaginary startups. I care about useful feedback, safe defaults, and the unglamorous details that make an idea runnable.
